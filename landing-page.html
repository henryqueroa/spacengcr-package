Common Questions

What does a bottleneck audit include?
We review your current tech stack, data flow, and manual processes. We identify the 3 highest-impact areas where automation or better infrastructure would save the most time and money. You get a written report with specific recommendations.
How long does a typical project take?
Most projects run 1 to 6 months depending on scope. A focused AI integration or data pipeline can be live in 4 to 6 weeks. Full infrastructure builds with multiple components typically take 3 to 6 months.
Do you work with companies outside Costa Rica?
Yes. We work with clients globally. Our team overlaps with US time zones and we communicate in English and Spanish.
What if we already have some infrastructure in place?
We build on top of what you have. We do not rip and replace unless it is necessary. Most clients have existing tools (CRMs, databases, spreadsheets) that we connect into a unified system.
What makes Space Engineering different from other dev shops?
We build the full stack. Most vendors sell one piece. We build the AI, the data pipeline that feeds it, and the cloud infrastructure that runs it. Everything works together from day one.
