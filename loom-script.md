# Evergreen Loom Script - Space Engineering

> Duration: 2-3 minutes
> Type: Cinematic Loom (screen recording with bespoke hook)
> Screen: Hub page open in browser

---

## Part 1: Hook (15 seconds) [BESPOKE]

"Hey David, Fiorella. This is Henryque from Zytrom. I spent a few hours researching Space Engineering and I put together something I think you will find interesting. Let me walk you through it."

## Part 2: Why You (15 seconds) [BESPOKE]

"I found Space Engineering because I was looking at tech companies in Costa Rica that do serious engineering work but have not built a marketing system around it yet. Your AI and data pipeline work is impressive. But when I looked at how people find you, I noticed some gaps. So I went ahead and built a few things."

## Part 3: Walk-Through (90-120 seconds) [SCREEN SHARE]

*Open hub page on screen. Scroll slowly through each section.*

"This is a page I built specifically for Space Engineering. Everything here is based on real research."

*Click open Research Snapshot accordion*

"First, I dug into your website, your LinkedIn, your public presence. I found 5 specific gaps in your current marketing. The two biggest ones: you have zero social proof visible on your website. No testimonials, no case studies, no client logos. For enterprise buyers, that is the number one reason they leave a website without reaching out. Second, you have no lead capture system. The only option for visitors is a contact form. Anyone who is interested but not ready to buy right now is lost."

*Click open Campaign Angles accordion*

"I wrote 5 campaign angles you could use in ads, emails, or LinkedIn content. Each one addresses a different pain point your clients have. For example, 'The Invisible Bottleneck' targets companies whose teams waste 40% of their week on manual data work."

*Click open Landing Page Preview accordion*

"Then I built you a complete landing page. This uses your exact brand identity. Your colors, your fonts, your team photos. It has a hero section, a problem section, services, a results section with your actual work, a lead magnet, your team bios, FAQs. It is live right now."

*Pause briefly*

"I also wrote ad scripts and a VSL outline. Those are in here too."

## Part 4: Close (15-20 seconds) [BACK TO CAMERA]

"Everything on this page is yours. Whether we work together or not. If you want to talk about implementing any of it, or if you want to see what a full marketing system for Space Engineering would look like, here is my calendar link. It is a 30-minute call, no pitch. Just a conversation."

*Point to calendar link on screen*

"Talk soon."

---

## Recording Notes

- Record the hook and "why you" parts looking at the camera. Keep it casual and confident.
- Switch to screen share for the walk-through. Move slowly. Let them see the details.
- Keep the total time under 3 minutes. People drop off after that.
- Do not read from a script. Use these points as a guide and speak naturally.
- Film in good lighting. Clean background. Professional but not stiff.
